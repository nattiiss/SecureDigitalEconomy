About Us

Who We Are

PrimeEvent Management is a full-service event agency based in Vienna, specialising in the conception, planning, and execution of corporate events. We support partners such as A1, Erste Group, Wiener Linien, and international organisations with tailored solutions that combine creativity with strong project management.

Our Mission

We believe that events are more than gatherings — they are strategic communication tools. Our mission is to create moments that inform, inspire, and connect people while ensuring seamless execution for our clients.

What We Do

Our services include strategic event consulting, concept development, participant management, venue selection, logistics, technical production, on-site coordination, and post-event analytics. We ensure reliable and efficient workflows for events of all sizes.

Our Clients

We partner with organisations from telecommunications, finance, public transport, technology, and the public sector. We maintain long-term relationships and design events that match their communication objectives.

Our Approach

We focus on transparency, agile planning, digital tools, clear communication, and quality assurance. Every event is unique — and our processes adapt to meet the specific goals of each client.
