NORDLICHT EVENT GMBH


General information

Nordlicht Event GmbH is a Vienna-based event service provider specialising in the planning, organisation, and execution of corporate events of all scales. We support leading companies such as A1, Erste Group, Wiener Linien, and international partners with tailored event concepts that combine creativity, precision, and technological expertise.

Whether it’s large-scale conferences, leadership workshops, product launches, or exclusive corporate gatherings — our team ensures seamless coordination from the first idea to the final implementation. With a strong focus on digital tools and modern communication workflows, we create efficient processes that allow our clients to focus on what matters most: their audience.
